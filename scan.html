gnulte-scan

The companion scanner. No root required.

GNULTE needs root to shape traffic. Discovering your network does not. gnulte-scan lists every active device and pulls the maximum detail out of each one.

What it reports
  • IP, MAC, OUI vendor and device type
  • Device names from up to five sources — mDNS, reverse DNS, NetBIOS and more
  • Gateway detected by IP and MAC
  • Open ports and OS fingerprint with --deep
  • Export as JSON, YAML or CSV
Usage
# device list, details on each $ gnulte-scan --deep # custom interface and subnet, CSV export $ gnulte-scan -i wlan0 -C 192.168.0.0/24 -c > devices.csv
