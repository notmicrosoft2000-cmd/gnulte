Features

Apply real network conditions to devices you own.

LAN scanning

Detects your interface, gateway and IP, then scans the network. Every device is fingerprinted — vendor, hostname, open ports and type.

Flexible targeting

One IP, several IPs, a whole subnet, or an IP resolved from a MAC. Whitelist your own devices and they are skipped automatically.

Traffic shaping

Traffic is routed through your machine via ARP spoof and shaped with tc netem — latency, jitter, loss, duplication, reordering and bandwidth, applied on the wire.

Live dashboard

Every target is pinged once per second during a test, with last, min, max, average and loss shown live.

Reports

Each run ends with a report folder containing SVG latency graphs and status summaries.

Self-healing

Spoof processes are monitored and restarted if they die. Cleanup always runs — qdisc removed, forwarding off.
