Features

Network testing the way network testing should be done.

Capabilities

What GNULTE does

LAN scanning

ARP-scans your network and fingerprints every device — OUI vendor, hostname, open ports and device type. Routers, phones, TVs, printers, cameras and IoT, all labelled and colour-coded by connection type.

Flexible targeting

Select one IP, several IPs, an entire subnet, or resolve a target from its MAC address. Whitelist your own devices and range tests skip them automatically.

Traffic shaping

IP forwarding on, ARP spoof routing the target through your machine, and tc netem applied on the wire: latency, jitter, loss, duplication, reordering and bandwidth. Bandwidth caps chain underneath the qdisc, so shaping is never lost.

Parameter validation

Every parameter is validated before it runs and its impact is estimated out loud before applying. It refuses to target the gateway or your own machine.

Live dashboard

Every target is pinged once per second during a run — last, min, max, average and loss — rendered live with a full-screen, full-data view.

Reports & capture

Every run ends in a report — gnulte-report-<ts>/ with SVG latency graphs. Capture a .pcap with --capture, or export per-second CSV with --export.

Self-healing

The spoof monitor watches arpspoof and revives it if it dies mid-test. Targets are verified reachable first, and cleanup always runs — qdisc removed, spoof processes killed, forwarding restored.

LAN hygiene

--dupcheck detects duplicate IPs and ARP conflicts; --stealth hides GNULTE's presence on systems you own; profiles and --save-profile reproduce conditions exactly.

Workflow

From scan to report

Four steps, a live dashboard in the middle, and a report on the way out.

STEP 01

Accept the disclaimer

Acceptance is persisted to ~/.gnulte_accepted and re-verified every session.

STEP 02

Scan and select

Interface, gateway and IP are detected, then the network is scanned. Pick a target, several, or a subnet.

STEP 03

Shape the link

Forwarding is enabled, arpspoof reroutes the target, and tc netem applies the conditions you chose.

STEP 04

Verify and clean up

The dashboard pings every second; when it's over, everything is removed and a report with SVG graphs is written.
